Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This policy describes the Android app Whiteboard (com.boardstudio.app), provided by Haider Ali Dhothar. Contact: haidersarfraz0323@gmail.com.

Your boards and media

Whiteboard stores boards, drawings, text, imported photos and videos, edited images, and preferences in the app’s storage on your device. The app does not operate a developer-hosted account or cloud-sync service. It accesses photos and videos you choose through Android’s file picker and copies selected media into app storage for use on your boards.

Photo processing

Background removal and related photo tools use Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation and Selfie Segmentation. Image processing takes place on your device. ML Kit does not send the input images or resulting cutouts to Google servers for this processing. Some features require Google Play services and a model download.

Google SDK diagnostics

Google ML Kit can collect device and application information, device or installation identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature versions, events, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this diagnostic data is encrypted in transit. See ML Kit’s data disclosures, ML Kit terms and privacy, and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing and support

When you export or share work, you choose its destination. The receiving app or service handles it under its own policy. If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send and use them to respond to your request. Avoid sending private board content unless needed for support.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until removed. Uninstalling Whiteboard or clearing its storage through Android settings removes its local app data. Exported files and copies shared with other apps remain in their respective locations and must be deleted separately. To request deletion of support correspondence, email the contact above. Google controls retention of its SDK diagnostic data under its policies.

Children and families

Whiteboard is intended for children and adults, including teaching and learning. The app does not ask users to create an account or provide their name, age, or contact information. Parents and guardians should supervise importing and sharing personal images or other sensitive content. Google SDK diagnostic processing described above may occur when photo tools are used. Contact us with any concern about a child’s information.

Security and changes

App content is stored using Android’s app storage protections. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change and will update the effective date on this page.
